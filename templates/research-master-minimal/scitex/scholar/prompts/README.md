# Scholar Prompts

AI-assisted prompts for literature review and bibliography management.

## Available Prompts

- `summarize_paper.md` - Summarize a research paper
- `compare_studies.md` - Compare multiple studies
- `citation_search.md` - Find relevant citations

## Usage

These prompts can be used with AI assistants to help with:
- Literature search and synthesis
- Understanding complex papers
- Building comprehensive bibliographies
