\caption{\textbf{Held-out confusion matrix for the linear support vector machine.}\\
\smallskip
Rows are the true digit, columns the predicted digit, over the 150 held-out
images (15 per class) of the 70/30 stratified split described in Section
\ref{sec:methods}. Cell counts are printed in the cells, so every claim in
Section \ref{sec:results} can be read off directly. Overall accuracy is
$131/150 = 87.3\%$. The diagonal is strong for nine of the ten classes; the
digit 8 is the exception at $7/15$, and its errors are spread over five other
classes rather than one, most often 1. Regenerate with
\texttt{python scripts/reproduce\_figures.py}.
}
\label{fig:02_confusion_matrix}
