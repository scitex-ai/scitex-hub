\caption{\textbf{The data at the resolution the classifier sees.}\\
\smallskip
One example of each digit 0--9, taken from \texttt{data/digits\_sample.csv} and
drawn directly from its pixel columns. Each image is $8\times8$ with integer
greyscale in $[0, 16]$, rendered dark-on-light. At this resolution strokes are
two to three pixels wide and several classes overlap heavily in which pixels
they light --- 8 against 1 most of all, which is where the classifier's errors
later concentrate (Figure \ref{fig:02_confusion_matrix}). Regenerate with
\texttt{python scripts/reproduce\_figures.py}.
}
\label{fig:01_digit_grid}
