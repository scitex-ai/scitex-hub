FAQ

What happens to my data during alpha?

No guarantee. Back up important work. For production, self-host or use pip install scitex locally.

Can I self-host SciTeX?

Yes. 100% open source under AGPL-3.0 license. Self-host on your own infrastructure for free.

When will paid plans be available?

After alpha phase. Timeline TBD based on stability and community feedback.

How can I support SciTeX now?

You can support SciTeX development through GitHub Sponsors or Buy Me a Coffee. Visit our donate page for details.
