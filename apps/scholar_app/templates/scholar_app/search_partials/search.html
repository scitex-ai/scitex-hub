Search Operators
Filter by field
-t neural → title contains "neural"
-a Smith → author is "Smith"
-j Nature → journal is "Nature"
Exclude with minus prefix
-t -mouse → title excludes "mouse"
-a -Jones → author is not "Jones"
Year & metrics range
-ymin 2020 -ymax 2024 → published 2020-2024
-cmin 100 → at least 100 citations
-ifmin 10 → impact factor ≥ 10
Full example
hippocampus -t human -t -mouse -a Smith -ymin 2020
→ Search "hippocampus", title has "human" but not "mouse", by Smith, since 2020
